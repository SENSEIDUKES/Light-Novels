## 2025-02-12 - [Zustand Pure Updaters & Concurrency]   **Learning:** The Zustand store's `updateChapter` method passes a callback to update state sequentially. Mutating external state from within this updater callback is an anti-pattern as the callback might be evaluated in multiple scenarios or discarded. **Action:** Use an external lock (`useRef` flag or `Set`) to track ongoing asynchronous events (like sealing) prior to executing them, and wrap them in a try-finally block to ensure they release securely regardless of success or failure.
